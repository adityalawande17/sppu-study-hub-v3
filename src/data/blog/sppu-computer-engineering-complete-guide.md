Computer Engineering (CS) is one of the largest and most popular branches at SPPU-affiliated colleges. Here's a complete walkthrough of what you'll study, how the patterns differ, and what the workload actually looks like.

## Overview

SPPU Computer Engineering spans three years of specialised study (SE, TE, BE) after the common First Year. The curriculum covers both theoretical CS fundamentals and practical engineering skills.

- **Affiliated colleges:** Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Nashik, Aurangabad, and across Maharashtra
- **Total credits:** Varies by regulation year, typically 180–200 for the full programme
- **Pattern years:** 2019 (for existing students) and 2024 (for new admissions from 2024)

## Second Year (SE) Subjects

The SE year establishes core CS fundamentals:

**2019 Pattern — SE CS**

- Discrete Mathematics
- Fundamentals of Data Structures
- Object Oriented Programming
- Computer Graphics
- Digital Electronics and Logic Design (Sem 3)
- Engineering Mathematics III
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Software Engineering
- Microprocessor
- Principles of Programming Languages (Sem 4)

**2024 Pattern — SE CS** introduces the PCC coding and slightly restructures the sequence — Data Structures and AI/ML concepts enter the SE year earlier.

## Third Year (TE) Subjects

TE is widely considered the most content-heavy year:

**2019 Pattern — TE CS**

- Database Management Systems
- Theory of Computation
- Systems Programming and Operating System
- Computer Networks and Security
- Data Science and Big Data Analytics
- Web Technology
- Artificial Intelligence
- Plus electives

These are the subjects most students find the most demanding — and the ones that carry the most marks in university exams. Previous year papers for DBMS, OS, and Computer Networks on this site show consistent question patterns across years.

## Final Year (BE) Subjects

BE focuses on electives, a project, and advanced topics:

- Machine Learning
- High Performance Computing
- Deep Learning
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Blockchain Technology
- 4–5 elective slots (varies by college and pattern)
- Major project (spans both semesters, heavily weighted)

The project is typically 150–200 credits' worth of weight in your final grade, making it critical to choose a topic you can actually complete.

## The 2024 Pattern Changes for CS

Under 2024, the course codes shift to the PCC/PEC system. The content is broadly similar for SE, with restructuring in elective offerings and some subject resequencing. If you're in SE 2024, your subjects are available as [Syllabus PDFs](/syllabus) — select 2024 and SE.

## Subjects Students Find Hardest

Based on KT rates and commonly asked exam questions, the consistently tough subjects in CS are:

1. **Theory of Computation (ToC)** — highly conceptual, grammar and automata derivations
2. **Computer Networks** — large syllabus, calculation-heavy protocols
3. **Engineering Mathematics III** — SE year, heavy on transforms and statistics
4. **Design and Analysis of Algorithms (DAA)** — complexity proofs in BE

These are the subjects to prioritise in terms of study hours and PYQ analysis.

## Career Paths from SPPU CS

SPPU Computer Engineering graduates typically go into:

- Software development (most common)
- Data engineering and analytics
- DevOps and cloud infrastructure
- System software and embedded development
- Postgraduate study (GATE, university abroad)

Campus placements vary significantly by college, but Pune's tech industry concentration means SPPU CS graduates have solid placement options across tier-1 and tier-2 companies.
