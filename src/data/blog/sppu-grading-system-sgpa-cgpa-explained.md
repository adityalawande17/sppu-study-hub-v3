SPPU uses a **10-point grading scale** for all engineering programmes. Understanding how your SGPA and CGPA are calculated helps you track your performance accurately — and avoid surprises on your marksheet.

## The Grade Scale

| Marks (out of 100) | Grade | Grade Point |
| ------------------ | ----- | ----------- |
| 90 – 100           | O     | 10          |
| 80 – 89            | A+    | 9           |
| 70 – 79            | A     | 8           |
| 60 – 69            | B+    | 7           |
| 50 – 59            | B     | 6           |
| 45 – 49            | C     | 5           |
| 40 – 44            | D     | 4           |
| Below 40           | F     | 0 (Fail)    |

Note: The passing marks for each subject vary — SPPU typically requires **40% in the external exam** and a combined passing mark in internal + external. Always check your specific subject's scheme.

## What Is SGPA?

**SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)** is the weighted average of grade points you earned in a single semester, weighted by each subject's credits.

**Formula:**

> SGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) / Σ Credits

**Example:**

| Subject  | Credits | Grade | Grade Points | Points × Credits |
| -------- | ------- | ----- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| DBMS     | 3       | A+    | 9            | 27               |
| OS       | 3       | A     | 8            | 24               |
| CN       | 3       | B+    | 7            | 21               |
| ToC      | 3       | A+    | 9            | 27               |
| Elective | 3       | B     | 6            | 18               |

SGPA = (27 + 24 + 21 + 27 + 18) / (3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3) = 117 / 15 = **7.8**

## What Is CGPA?

**CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)** is the weighted average across all semesters completed so far. It uses the same formula but pools all subjects from all completed semesters.

> CGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) across all semesters / Σ Credits across all semesters

Your CGPA on your final marksheet is what appears on your degree certificate and is what most employers and universities look at.

## Converting CGPA to Percentage

SPPU uses this official conversion formula:

> **Percentage = CGPA × 9.5**

So a CGPA of 8.2 = **77.9%**. This conversion is printed on your final transcript, but you'll need it for applications that ask for a percentage directly.

## Class / Division

| CGPA       | Class                        |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- |
| 9.0+       | First Class with Distinction |
| 7.5 – 8.99 | First Class                  |
| 6.0 – 7.49 | Second Class                 |
| 5.0 – 5.99 | Pass Class                   |
| Below 5.0  | Fail (KT)                    |

## Internal Marks vs External Marks

Most subjects are split **20 (internal) + 80 (external)**. You need to:

- Pass the **external exam independently** (typically 32 out of 80)
- Pass the **combined total** (typically 40 out of 100)

Failing only the external — even if internal marks are high — counts as an **F grade** and you'll need to appear for the supplementary exam.
