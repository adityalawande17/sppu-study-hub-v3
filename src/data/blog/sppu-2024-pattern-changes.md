The Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) rolled out its **2024 curriculum pattern** for engineering, replacing the 2019 pattern for new admissions. If you're currently in SE, TE, or BE — here's what actually changed and what it means for how you study.

## New Course Code System

The most visible change is the course coding system. Where 2019 used purely numeric codes (like `210241` for Discrete Mathematics in SE CS), the 2024 pattern uses descriptive alphanumeric codes:

- **PCC** — Programme Core Course
- **PEC** — Programme Elective Course
- **MDM** — Mandatory / Department Module
- **OE** — Open Elective

So instead of memorising `310241`, you'd see `PCC-301-CST` — which already tells you it's a core course, third-year, first semester, Computer Science. Much easier to track once you know the system.

## Credit Distribution

The 2024 pattern moves toward an **Outcome-Based Education (OBE)** framework more explicitly than 2019 did. Each subject now has clearly stated Programme Outcomes (POs) that the course is expected to fulfil.

Credit counts have also been adjusted — most theory subjects are 3 credits, with lab courses listed as separate 1–2 credit entries rather than bundled with the theory.

## Branch-Specific Changes

### Computer Engineering and IT

SE semesters for CS and IT now include clearly restructured subjects. Operating Systems, Data Structures, and AI & ML appear earlier in the sequence, and elective slots are more clearly labelled as PEC (Programme Elective Course).

### AI & DS and AI & ML

These branches have the most restructured syllabuses under 2024, with focused AI/ML subjects replacing some of the older general engineering courses from 2019.

### Mechanical and Civil

As of mid-2025, the 2024 pattern syllabus for Mechanical and Civil branches is still being finalised by SPPU for higher years (TE and BE). SE syllabuses are available on the SPPU circular portal.

## What This Means for You

- If you're in **FE (First Year)**: 2024 pattern is already in effect — one common syllabus for all branches.
- If you're in **SE**: your branch's 2024 pattern subjects are now defined. Check the [Syllabus PDFs](/syllabus) page for your branch PDF.
- If you're in **TE or BE on 2019 pattern**: you continue on 2019 until graduation. No mid-stream switch.
