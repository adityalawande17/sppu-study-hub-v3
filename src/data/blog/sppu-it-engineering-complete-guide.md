Information Technology (IT) Engineering at SPPU is often compared directly to Computer Engineering. They share significant overlap in subjects, but have distinct specialisations in the later years. Here's the full picture.

## IT vs CS — What's Actually Different

The two branches have largely the same SE curriculum. The divergence begins at TE, where IT emphasises:

- **Information management and systems** over low-level OS and compiler design
- **Software project management** as a core subject
- **Data science and analytics** earlier in the curriculum
- **Web and application development** over hardware-adjacent topics

By the time you're in BE, the two branches look quite different in their elective offerings. IT leans toward applications; CS leans toward systems and theory.

## Second Year (SE) Subjects — IT

SE IT is almost identical to SE CS in content. The core maths, data structures, and digital electronics foundation is shared.

**2019 Pattern — SE IT:**

- Data Structures (similar to CS but different code)
- Database Management Systems
- Computer Networks fundamentals
- Web Technology (introduced earlier than in CS)
- Engineering Mathematics III
- Elective options

**2024 Pattern — SE IT:**
Under the new 2024 pattern, SE IT introduces `PCC-` coded subjects. Operating System, Data Structures, and Artificial Intelligence appear explicitly in the SE year — aligning with industry demand for AI/ML exposure earlier.

## Third Year (TE) Subjects — IT

This is where IT distinguishes itself most clearly:

**2019 Pattern — TE IT:**

- Operating System
- Computer Networks
- Software Engineering
- Web-based Application Development
- Data Science and Big Data Analytics
- Advanced topics in information management

**2024 Pattern — TE IT:**
The 2024 TE IT subjects (added to this site recently) include:

- PCC-301-ITT: Operating System
- PCC-302-ITT: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- PCC-303-ITT: Theory of Computation
- PCC-351-ITT: Data Science & Big Data Analytics
- PCC-352-ITT: Software Engineering & Project Management
- Plus two Program Elective slots (PEC-321-ITT and PEC-361/362-ITT)

AI & ML appearing explicitly in TE IT under 2024 is a significant change from 2019, where it was an elective. You can [browse the full TE IT 2024 subject list](/branches/it).

## Final Year (BE) Subjects — IT

BE IT focuses on project work and elective specialisations. Common elective streams include:

- Cloud computing and DevOps
- Information security and cybersecurity
- Machine learning applications
- Big data and analytics pipelines
- IoT and embedded systems

The BE project is the largest single assessment component — choose a topic that intersects industry needs with something you can actually complete in two semesters.

## Subjects Students Find Hardest in IT

1. **Theory of Computation** — appears in TE IT 2024 for the first time; automata and grammars are abstract
2. **Computer Networks** — large syllabus, protocol-heavy
3. **Data Science & Big Data Analytics** — maths-heavy, statistics background required
4. **Engineering Mathematics III** — the SE-year hurdle that affects many students' SGPA

## Placement and Career Paths

IT Engineering graduates have strong placement options in Pune, given the city's software services concentration. Typical roles:

- Full-stack and backend development (Node.js, Java, Python)
- Business analysis and systems analysis
- Database administration and data engineering
- QA and test automation
- Cloud and infrastructure engineering

Many IT graduates also pursue MBA or MCA paths, where the systems and management angle of the IT curriculum is an advantage over pure CS.

## Is IT or CS Better?

Both are strong choices at SPPU. If you're interested in low-level systems, theory of computation, and research, CS has a slight edge. If you're more interested in applications, information management, and project-oriented work, IT aligns better.

In terms of placement outcomes, both branches perform similarly in Pune's market.
