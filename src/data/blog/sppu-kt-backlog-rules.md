A **KT (Keep Term)** at SPPU means you've failed a subject — your term is kept, meaning the subject carries forward and you must clear it before graduating. Here's everything you need to know.

## What Counts as a KT?

You get a KT in a subject if:

- You score below the passing marks in the **external exam** (typically 32/80)
- You score below the combined passing mark (typically 40/100 including internals)
- You were **detained** due to attendance below 75%

The F grade on your marksheet = one KT. Each subject is counted independently.

## How Many KTs Are Allowed?

SPPU has a cap on how many KTs you can carry and still be eligible to move to the next year:

| Situation              | KT Limit                                            |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Move from SE to TE     | Maximum **4 KTs** from SE (both semesters combined) |
| Move from TE to BE     | Maximum **4 KTs** from TE (both semesters combined) |
| Graduation eligibility | All KTs from **all years must be cleared**          |

> These limits may vary slightly based on the specific regulation year. Always verify with your college's exam department.

If you have more than 4 KTs from a year, you may be required to repeat that academic year — your college will notify you.

## When Can You Clear a KT?

SPPU offers **supplementary exams** (also called KT exams) once or twice a year:

- **After the winter exams** (November–December): supplementary usually held in March–April
- **After the summer exams** (April–May): supplementary usually held in October–November

The exact schedule is announced on the [SPPU exam portal](https://exam.unipune.ac.in). You apply for the KT exam through your college, which submits the form to the university before the deadline.

## Internal Marks in KT Exams

When you appear for a KT exam, your **internal marks from the original semester are carried forward** — you don't redo internals. Only the external exam (80 marks) is re-attempted.

This is an advantage: if you had decent internals (say 15/20), you only need 25/80 externally to pass combined — though you still need the external minimum separately.

## ATKT — Allowed to Keep Term

ATKT stands for **Allowed to Keep Term**. If you appear for the exam despite having a KT from a previous semester (within the allowed limit), you're an ATKT student. Most colleges track this automatically based on your KT count.

## Strategies to Clear KTs Fast

**1. Don't leave KTs to pile up.** One KT cleared early is much less stressful than three KTs in your final year.

**2. Study the question paper pattern.** KT exams usually follow the same pattern as regular exams. Previous year KT papers are similar to regular exam papers — same units, same weightage.

**3. Focus on passing, not scoring.** For KT papers, your goal is clearing, not distinction. Target the high-frequency units, attempt internal options you're confident about, and ensure you cross the external minimum.

**4. Check your marksheet carefully.** Sometimes a paper gets re-evaluated or a calculation error occurs. If you genuinely feel you deserved more, apply for re-verification within the deadlines SPPU publishes after result day.

## Checking Your KT Status

Your marksheet shows F grades clearly. You can also check your KT status through the official SPPU result portal at [results.unipune.ac.in](https://results.unipune.ac.in) using your exam seat number.
